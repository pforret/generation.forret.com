# Charli D'Amelio

> **Charli D'Amelio** was born in **2004**, making them a member of the Generation Z. Field: Personality.

![Charli D'Amelio](../img/people/charli-damelio.jpg)

Charli Grace D'Amelio (born May 1, 2004) is an American social media personality and dancer. She was a competitive dancer for over a decade before she came to prominence in late 2019, when she began posting dance videos on the video-sharing platform TikTok and joined The Hype House that same year. D'Amelio quickly amassed a large following and subsequently became the most-followed creator on the platform from March 2020 to June 2022. With over 158 million followers, she is the second most-followed person on TikTok as of 2026.
