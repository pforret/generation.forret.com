# Margot Robbie

> **Margot Robbie** was born in **1990**, making them a member of the Generation Y. Field: Actor.

![Margot Robbie](../img/people/margot-robbie.jpg)

Margot Elise Robbie (born 2 July 1990) is an Australian actress and producer. The world's highest-paid actress in 2023, she is known for her performances in both blockbuster and independent films. Robbie's accolades include seven AACTA Awards and three Critics' Choice Awards in addition to nominations for three Academy Awards, six British Academy Film Awards, and four Golden Globes.
