# George H.W. Bush

> **George H.W. Bush** was born in **1924**, making them a member of the Greatest Generation. Field: Politics.

![George H.W. Bush](../img/people/george-hw-bush.jpg)

George Herbert Walker Bush (June 12, 1924 – November 30, 2018) was the 41st president of the United States, serving from 1989 to 1993. A member of the Republican Party, he served as the 43rd vice president under Ronald Reagan. His son, George W. Bush, served as the 43rd president.
