# Lil Nas X

> **Lil Nas X** was born in **1999**, making them a member of the Generation Z. Field: Musician.

![Lil Nas X](../img/people/lil-nas-x.jpg)

Montero Lamar Hill (born April 9, 1999), better known by his stage name Lil Nas X, is an American rapper, singer, and songwriter. He rose to prominence with the release of his 2018 country rap single "Old Town Road", which spent a then-record 19 weeks atop the U.S. Billboard Hot 100 chart. Simultaneously, he came out as gay, the first artist to do so while having a number-one record.
