# Zendaya

> **Zendaya** was born in **1996**, making them a member of the Generation Y. Field: Actor.

![Zendaya](../img/people/zendaya.jpg)

Zendaya Maree Stoermer Coleman (born September 1, 1996) is an American actress and singer. Known for her work in television and blockbusters, her films as a leading actress have grossed over $9.8 billion worldwide. Her accolades include two Primetime Emmy Awards and a Golden Globe. In 2022, Time named her one of the 100 most influential people in the world.
