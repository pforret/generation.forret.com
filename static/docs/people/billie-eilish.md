# Billie Eilish

> **Billie Eilish** was born in **2001**, making them a member of the Generation Z. Field: Musician.

![Billie Eilish](../img/people/billie-eilish.jpg)

Billie Eilish Pirate Baird O'Connell (born December 18, 2001) is an American singer-songwriter. Known for her distinctive musical sound and vocal style, she is a prominent figure in 2020s pop culture. Eilish's production style frequently uses harmony lines and vocal layering, an extension of double tracking.
