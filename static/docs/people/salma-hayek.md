# Salma Hayek

> **Salma Hayek** was born in **1966**, making them a member of the Generation X. Field: Actor.

![Salma Hayek](../img/people/salma-hayek.jpg)

Salma Valgarma Hayek Pinault (née Hayek Jiménez; born September 2, 1966) is a Mexican and American actress and film producer. She began her career in Mexico with starring roles in the telenovela Teresa (1989–1991) as well as the romantic drama Midaq Alley (1995). She soon established herself in Hollywood with appearances in films such as Desperado (1995), From Dusk till Dawn (1996), Wild Wild West (1999), and Dogma (1999).
