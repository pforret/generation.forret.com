# Jenna Ortega

> **Jenna Ortega** was born in **2002**, making them a member of the Generation Z. Field: Actor.

![Jenna Ortega](../img/people/jenna-ortega.jpg)

Jenna Marie Ortega (born September 27, 2002) is an American actress. Known for her work in horror, Ortega has been called "Gen Z's scream queen" by media publications. She was featured on The Hollywood Reporter's Power 100 list in 2023 and the Forbes 30 Under 30 list in 2024.
