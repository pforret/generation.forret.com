# Justin Bieber

> **Justin Bieber** was born in **1994**, making them a member of the Generation Y. Field: Musician.

![Justin Bieber](../img/people/justin-bieber.jpg)

Justin Drew Bieber (born March 1, 1994) is a Canadian singer. An influential figure in popular music, he is known for his image reinventions and multi-genre performances. With estimated sales of 150 million records, he is one of the best-selling music artists of all time.
