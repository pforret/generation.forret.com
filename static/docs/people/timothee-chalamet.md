# Timothée Chalamet

> **Timothée Chalamet** was born in **1995**, making them a member of the Generation Y. Field: Actor.

![Timothée Chalamet](../img/people/timothee-chalamet.jpg)

Timothée Hal Chalamet (born December 27, 1995) is an American and French actor. Known for his work in a diverse range of films, he is the recipient of numerous accolades including an Actor Award, a Golden Globe Award, and two Critics' Choice Awards, in addition to nominations for four Academy Awards, six BAFTAs, and a Grammy Award. His films as a leading actor have grossed over US$2.3 billion worldwide.
